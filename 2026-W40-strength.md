# Strength & Core – Week 2026-W40

First block for a new lifter (`strengthExperience: new`, `strengthRecentFrequency: none`): light load, higher reps, focus on control and pattern — not the standard working-load prescriptions. Your reported 50 kg squat/deadlift is noted as the eventual target to build back toward over the next 2-3 weeks, not this week's load. | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load / cue | Rest |
